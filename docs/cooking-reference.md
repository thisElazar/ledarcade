## 1. The Five French Mother Sauces

### Bechamel (White Sauce)
- **Base:** White roux + milk
- **Ratio:** 1:1 butter:flour (by weight), ~2 Tbsp each per 1 cup milk (medium)
- **Technique:** Melt butter, whisk flour, cook 2-3 min (white roux). Gradually whisk in milk. Simmer 10-15 min. Season: salt, white pepper, nutmeg.
- **Daughters:** Mornay (+Gruyere/Parmesan), Soubise (+pureed onion), Nantua (+crayfish butter), Cream/Creme (+heavy cream), Mustard (+Dijon), Aurore (+tomato puree)

### Veloute (Blond Sauce)
- **Base:** Blond roux + white stock (chicken, veal, or fish)
- **Ratio:** 1:1 butter:flour, cooked 4-5 min to pale gold
- **Technique:** Make blond roux, whisk in warm stock. Simmer 30-45 min, skimming. Strain.
- **Daughters:** Supreme (+cream+butter+lemon, chicken), Allemande (+egg yolk+lemon+cream, veal), Bercy (+shallots+white wine+parsley, fish), Normandy (+mushrooms+white wine, fish), Hungarian (+onions+paprika+wine)

### Espagnole (Brown Sauce)
- **Base:** Brown roux + brown stock + tomato puree + mirepoix
- **Ratio:** 1:1 butter:flour, cooked 6-8 min to nut-brown
- **Technique:** Brown mirepoix. Add flour, cook to brown roux. Add stock and tomato. Simmer hours, skimming. Strain.
- **Demi-glace:** Equal parts Espagnole + brown stock, reduced by half.
- **Daughters (from demi-glace):** Bordelaise (+red wine+shallots+bone marrow), Chasseur/Hunter (+mushrooms+white wine+tomato), Robert (+onions+white wine+mustard), Madeira (+Madeira wine), Mushroom (+sauteed mushrooms), Charcutiere (Robert+cornichon strips)

### Hollandaise
- **Base:** Egg yolks + clarified butter + acid (lemon/white wine vinegar)
- **Ratio:** 3 yolks : 8 oz clarified butter : 1 Tbsp lemon juice
- **Technique:** Whisk yolks+water over bain-marie until thick and pale. Slowly drizzle warm clarified butter, whisking constantly. Season: lemon, salt, cayenne. Hold 145-155F.
- **Daughters:** Bearnaise (+tarragon+vinegar+shallot reduction), Choron (Bearnaise+tomato puree), Maltaise (+blood orange juice/zest), Mousseline/Chantilly (+whipped cream folded in)

### Tomato Sauce (Sauce Tomat)
- **Classical:** Sweat mirepoix+salt pork in butter. Light roux. Add crushed tomatoes, puree, stock, bouquet garni. Simmer 1-2 hrs. Strain.
- **Modern:** Saute aromatics in olive oil, add crushed tomatoes, simmer 30-60 min.
- **Daughters:** Marinara (+garlic+oregano+parsley), Bolognese/Ragu (+ground meat+soffritto+wine+milk), Creole (+holy trinity+cayenne), Puttanesca (+capers+olives+anchovies+garlic)

## 2. World Sauces

### Asian
- **Teriyaki:** 2 soy : 2 sake : 2 mirin : 1 sugar. Simmer until glossy. Optional ginger, garlic.
- **Ponzu:** Equal parts soy + citrus juice (yuzu/lemon/lime) + mirin. Traditional: steep with bonito+kombu overnight, strain.
- **Thai Peanut:** 1/2c PB + 2T soy + 1T rice vinegar + 1T sesame oil + 1T brown sugar + lime + garlic + ginger + chili. Thin with coconut milk.
- **Stir-Fry (general):** 3T soy + 1T oyster sauce + 1t sesame oil + 1t cornstarch slurry + pinch sugar.
- **Nuoc Cham (Vietnamese):** Equal parts fish sauce, lime juice, water, sugar + garlic + chili.

### Latin American
- **Chimichurri:** 1c parsley + 3-4 garlic + 2T oregano + 1/3c red wine vinegar + 1/2c olive oil + red pepper flakes. Rest 20+ min.
- **Mole Poblano:** 20+ ingredients: multiple dried chiles (ancho, mulato, pasilla, chipotle), chocolate, almonds/peanuts, sesame, tomatoes, tomatillos, raisins, plantain, cinnamon, cloves, cumin, oregano, onion, garlic. Toast, rehydrate, blend, simmer 1-2 hrs.
- **Salsa Verde:** 1 lb tomatillos (roasted) + serrano/jalapeno + onion + garlic + cilantro + lime + salt. Blend.
- **Sofrito (PR/Caribbean):** Onion + garlic + bell pepper (aji dulce) + tomato + cilantro + culantro + olive oil. Blend raw or cook down.

### Italian
- **Pesto Genovese:** 2c basil + 1/3c pine nuts (toasted) + 2-3 garlic + 1/2c Parmigiano + 1/2-3/4c EVOO. Mortar and pestle or pulse in processor.
- **Marinara:** EVOO + 4-5 garlic (sliced) + 28oz San Marzano tomatoes (hand-crushed) + salt + basil. Simmer 20-30 min. Simplicity is the point.
- **Aglio e Olio:** 1/3c olive oil + 6 garlic (thin-sliced) + 1/2t red pepper flakes + pasta + pasta water + parsley. Gentle garlic, emulsify with starchy water.

### Indian
- **Tikka Masala Base:** Saute onion until golden. Ginger-garlic paste 1 min. Spices: 1t each cumin, coriander, turmeric, paprika, garam masala + chili. Bloom 30 sec. Tomato puree, simmer 15-20 min. Finish with cream or coconut cream. Optional cashew paste.
- **Raita:** 1c yogurt + 1/2c cucumber + toasted cumin + salt + mint/cilantro. Chill.
- **Green Chutney:** 1c cilantro + 1/2c mint + green chiles + lemon + cumin + salt + water. Blend bright and sharp.
- **Tadka (Tempering):** Heat ghee, add whole spices (mustard seeds, cumin, curry leaves, dried red chiles) until pop. Pour sizzling over finished dish. Technique as much as sauce.

## 3. Flavor Profiles

### Salt, Fat, Acid, Heat (Samin Nosrat)
- **Salt:** Enhances, amplifies. Balances bitterness, brings out sweetness. Layer throughout cooking.
- **Fat:** Carries flavor (fat-soluble compounds). Creates mouthfeel. Each fat has distinct character.
- **Acid:** Brightens, balances, cuts richness. Sources: citrus, vinegar, wine, tomatoes, yogurt, fermentation.
- **Heat:** Determines texture. Low+slow for collagen. High+fast for Maillard crust. Dry vs wet heat.
- **Rule:** Flat → needs acid. Dull → needs salt. Lacks richness → needs fat. Texture wrong → adjust heat.

### Regional Profiles
- **Thai:** Lemongrass, galangal, Thai basil, fish sauce, lime, coconut milk, kaffir lime, chiles, palm sugar, shrimp paste. Balance: sweet+sour+salty+spicy.
- **Mexican:** Cumin, chili powder (ancho, guajillo, chipotle), Mexican oregano, lime, garlic, cilantro, tomatoes, avocado.
- **Indian:** Turmeric, cumin, coriander, cardamom, cinnamon, garam masala, fenugreek, ginger, garlic, yogurt, coconut milk, tamarind, saffron.
- **French:** Butter, shallots, thyme, tarragon, bay, chervil, chives, Herbes de Provence, Dijon, cream, wine, mushrooms, truffle.
- **Italian:** Garlic, basil, oregano, rosemary, parsley, EVOO, tomatoes, Parmigiano, balsamic, pine nuts, red pepper flakes, fennel, lemon.
- **Japanese:** Soy, miso, mirin, sake, rice vinegar, dashi (kombu+bonito), sesame, wasabi, ginger, nori.
- **Chinese:** Soy, sesame oil, chili oil, ginger, garlic, Sichuan peppercorn, star anise, five-spice, hoisin, oyster sauce, black bean paste.
- **Korean:** Gochujang, gochugaru, sesame oil, soy, garlic, ginger, kimchi, rice wine.
- **Middle Eastern:** Sumac, za'atar, cumin, cinnamon, turmeric, mint, parsley, tahini, pomegranate, yogurt, saffron, honey.
- **North African/Moroccan:** Ras el hanout, harissa, preserved lemon, saffron, cumin, cinnamon, ginger, mint, cilantro, honey, almonds.
- **Caribbean:** Allspice, scotch bonnet, jerk seasoning, nutmeg, cinnamon, cloves, rum, coconut, lime.
- **Spanish:** EVOO, saffron, smoked paprika (pimenton), chorizo, sherry vinegar, garlic, almonds, Manchego.

### Umami
- **Glutamate sources:** Parmesan, soy sauce, fish sauce, miso, tomatoes (cooked/concentrated), kombu, nutritional yeast, fermented foods.
- **Nucleotide sources (IMP/GMP):** Dried shiitake (GMP), bonito/katsuobushi (IMP), dried anchovies (IMP), aged meats (IMP).
- **Synergy (glutamate + nucleotide = up to 8x amplification):** Dashi (kombu+bonito), Italian (Parmesan+tomato+anchovy), mushroom risotto (Parmesan+porcini).
- **Tricks:** Splash of soy/fish sauce in non-Asian dishes. Miso in soups/braises. Tomato paste as base. Anchovy paste melted into sauces.

## 4. Baking Fundamentals

### Baker's Percentages (flour = 100%)

**Standard bread:**
| Ingredient | % |
|------------|---|
| Flour | 100% |
| Water | 60-65% |
| Salt | 2% |
| Yeast (instant) | 1% |
| Fat (optional) | 1-5% |

**Hydration guide:**
- 50-57% — Bagels, pretzels (stiff, dense, chewy)
- 58-65% — Standard bread and rolls
- 65-70% — Ciabatta, baguettes (open crumb)
- 70-80% — High-hydration artisan
- 80%+ — Focaccia, very wet doughs

**Pizza by style:**
- Neapolitan: 58-65% hydration, 2.5-3% salt, 0.1-0.3% yeast (long ferment)
- New York: ~65%, +2-3% olive oil, +1-2% sugar
- Detroit: ~65%, higher fat

### Core Ratios (by weight)
| Product | Ratio |
|---------|-------|
| Pound Cake | 1 flour : 1 butter : 1 sugar : 1 egg |
| Pie Dough | 3 flour : 2 fat : 1 water |
| Cookie Dough | 3 flour : 2 fat : 1 sugar |
| Muffins/Quick Bread | 2 flour : 2 liquid : 1 egg : 1 fat |
| Biscuits | 3 flour : 2 liquid : 1 fat |
| Pancakes | 2 flour : 2 liquid : 1 egg : 1/2 fat |
| Crepes | 1 flour : 1 egg : 1 liquid |

### Techniques
- **Creaming:** Beat softened butter+sugar 3-8 min until pale/fluffy. Traps air for leavening. Add eggs one at a time. Alternate dry/wet, start and end dry. Don't overmix.
- **Folding:** Incorporate light into heavy without deflating. Spatula: cut center, sweep bottom, fold top. Rotate 90°. Minimal strokes.
- **Proofing:** Bulk ferment (double, 1-2 hrs RT or 8-24 hrs cold). Shape. Final proof (poke test: slow partial springback = ready).
- **Autolyse:** Flour+water only, rest 20-60 min before salt/yeast. Hydrates flour, reduces kneading.

### Troubleshooting
| Problem | Cause |
|---------|-------|
| Dense cake | Overmixed, oven too cool, insufficient creaming |
| Cake sinks | Underbaked, too much leavening, opened oven early |
| Tough cookies | Too much flour, overmixed |
| Flat cookies | Butter too warm, too little flour, old leavening |
| Bread won't rise | Dead yeast, water too hot (>120F killed it) |
| Gummy bread | Underbaked (needs 190-210F internal) |
| Tough pie crust | Overworked dough, not enough fat |

## 5. Building Blocks

### Aromatic Bases
- **Mirepoix (French):** 2 onion : 1 carrot : 1 celery (by weight). Sweat in butter, no browning. Used in stocks, soups, braises.
- **White Mirepoix:** Parsnip/extra celery for carrot. No color. White stocks, cream soups.
- **Holy Trinity (Cajun/Creole):** 1-2 onion : 1 celery : 1 green bell pepper. Gumbo, jambalaya, etouffee.
- **Soffritto (Italian):** Onion+carrot+celery, minced fine. Slow-cooked in EVOO until sweet. Ragu, risotto.
- **Sofrito (Spanish/Latin):** Onion+garlic+tomato+bell pepper in EVOO. Puerto Rican adds culantro, aji dulce.

### Herb Bundles
- **Bouquet Garni:** Parsley stems + thyme sprigs + bay leaf. Tied with twine. Simmer in liquid, remove before service.
- **Sachet d'Epices:** Same herbs + peppercorns + optional cloves/garlic. Wrapped in cheesecloth (contains small loose spices).

### Roux
| Type | Time | Color | Flavor | Thickening | Used In |
|------|------|-------|--------|------------|---------|
| White | 2-3 min | Pale | Neutral | Strongest | Bechamel, cream soups |
| Blond | 4-5 min | Pale gold | Slight nutty | Strong | Veloute |
| Brown | 6-15 min | Med-dark brown | Nutty/toasty | Weakest | Espagnole, gumbo |

**Thickness per 1 cup liquid:** Thin=1T+1T, Medium=2T+2T, Thick=3T+3T (fat+flour)

### Stocks
- **General ratio:** 50-60% bones : 100% water : 10% mirepoix. ~2 lbs bones per 1 gal water.
- **White (chicken/veal):** Raw bones+cold water+mirepoix+sachet. Never boil (cloudy). Skim. Chicken 3-4 hrs, veal 6-8 hrs.
- **Brown (beef/veal):** Roast bones 400-425F until deep brown. Roast mirepoix. Add tomato paste last 15 min. Deglaze pan. Cold water, sachet. Simmer 8-12 hrs.
- **Fish (Fumet):** Lean white fish bones only (no oily fish). Sweat bones+white mirepoix in butter 5-10 min. White wine, cold water. 30-45 min ONLY (turns bitter).
- **Vegetable:** Onion, carrot, celery, leek, mushroom stems, parsley, peppercorns. No brassicas (sulfurous). 30-45 min only.
- **Remouillage:** Second extraction from used bones. Use as water for next stock batch.
- **Glace de Viande:** Stock reduced 90% to syrupy concentrate. Melts into sauces for instant depth.
