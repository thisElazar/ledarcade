# Sources

Where the catalog's factual claims come from. Generated by `tools/build_sources.py` from `tools/sources_ledger.json`; edit the ledger, not this file.

- 66 items, 67 claims
- **28 correct**, **28 imprecise**, **8 not_an_attribution**, **2 unverifiable**, **1 wrong**
- 63 read against a fetched source and dated; 4 not
- 34 lines have a correction proposed; 28 items implement something other than what they credit

"Credit" is the attribution line the cabinet and the Field Guide show. "Data" is a table typed or generated from somewhere else. "Experiment" is a result of our own.

**How this was checked.** Verified 2026-09-22 by a 17-agent sweep: 8 researchers, one adversarial checker per batch re-fetching every URL, and a completeness critic. 10 of 62 rulings overturned the researcher. No citation was written from memory: a claim nobody could settle from a primary source is recorded as unverifiable with an empty source, not filled in.

**Coverage.** Seeded 2026-09-22 from the 56 catalog entries that carry a 'credit', plus 6 items whose data comes from outside the repo and which carry no credit at all. Extended 2026-09-24 with 5 data-provenance entries for items that assert nothing checkable in a credit field but whose data tables come from identifiable traditions. Not covered: the 236 paintings and 70 wondercabinet titles (their provenance is in the gallery data, not in code), the 48 demos (they replay their game's own claims), and every item that asserts nothing checkable.
