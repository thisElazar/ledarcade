# What's new

Changes to the Wonder Cabinet software, newest first. Your cabinet shows
"update available" in the menu when a new version is out; install it from the
UPDATE menu.

## Unreleased

### New
- **FORTRESS**: a castle-siege game. Pick a castle, then each round place
  cannons, shell the ships bombarding your walls, and patch the breaches before
  the clock runs out. Leave a castle open to the sea and you surrender.
- **MOVEMENT** grows from three motions to 26, in three groups. GAIT has ten
  ways of getting about, from WALK and SPRINT to SNEAK and MOONWALK. EXERCISE
  has nine, from JUMP JACKS to BURPEE. WALL has seven done against a wall, such
  as SIDLE, PEEK, and WALL JUMP. Up/Down switches group.
- **YOGA** adds MOON SALUTE and INVERSION flows and ten new poses, and SUN
  SALUTE now runs through Plank, Cobra, and Downward Dog.
- **PIANO ROLL** now plays real music: eight pieces by Satie, Bach, Beethoven,
  Chopin, Sousa, and Joplin, on a 64-key roll that shows what each hand is
  doing.
- **MUSIC BOX** plays Scott Joplin's "The Entertainer", note for note, on a
  comb cut for the tune.
- **DRUM MACHINE** steps through 16 core beats and styles, from a plain
  backbeat to a blast beat, in place of patterns from specific songs.
- **GRAND MUSEUM** hides a secret switch room behind a wall of its starting
  chamber. Inside are a PARTY button, which turns the room into a disco, and
  the AFTER HOURS lever.
- **AFTER HOURS** lists the cabinet's mature items, which appear nowhere while
  it is off. Pulling the lever needs the eight-move stick code from your
  owner's card, and it switches itself off whenever the cabinet restarts. The
  menu's separator lines turn red while it is on.
- **DOOM**: Freedoom, the free Doom, on the cabinet's panel, with its own
  status display, a GAME / SKILL / MAP menu, an autosave and three save files,
  and a demo that plays itself. It is a mature item, so it is listed only while
  AFTER HOURS is on, and only on cabinets that have the Doom engine installed.

### Fixed
- YOGA's Downward Dog and Bound Angle were drawn nearly twice the size of
  every other pose, and the figure pinched to a line when moving between some
  poses.
- The website's timeline dated SNAKE from Nokia's 1997 phone game. It now
  dates it from Blockade (1976), the arcade game that started the genre.

## v1.1.1 — September 25, 2026

- **CREDITS** now lists every source the cabinet draws on, checked against the
  original sources.
- **COINS** has been removed while its photographs are replaced. It will come
  back in a later update.

## v1.1 — September 25, 2026

### New
- **MIRROR**: watch your cabinet's screen live in a web browser on the same
  network, with up to four viewers at a time. It's off by default. Turn it on in
  Settings.
- **LIFE LAB**: load a seed pattern, pick a rule, and set it running.
- **JELLYFISH LAB**, plus all 256 of **WOLFRAM**'s rules.
- **GRIDLOCK** now runs its traffic model on a real street grid.
- **RADIOACTIVE** has been rebuilt to show whole decay chains.
- **WIND TUNNEL** adds TESLA REV, which is the Tesla valve installed backwards.
- **MOLECULES** gains methanol, L-DOPA, and noradrenaline, and most of its 3D
  shapes now come from PubChem's reference structures.
- **PAINT** remembers your last tool, and DUP can copy to the next or last
  frame. **PAINT GIF** adds a SCRUB tool for flicking through frames, and undo
  goes further back.
- **USB SHARE**: plug in a USB stick to back up your paintings or swap them
  with another cabinet. It only appears while a stick is plugged in.
- Some games now show a stat line on the game-over screen.
- The Field Guide explains how to play every game and what each on-screen label
  means.

### Improved
- Demo modes play better. FLAPPY, FROGGY, STICK RUNNER, ASTROIDS, and 3D MONSTR
  MAZE now plan ahead instead of dying at random.
- FROGGY's turtles give you 3 seconds of warning before they dive.
- SEISMIC waves now bend through the Earth, and TECTONIC's plates actually move.
- The GRAY-SCOTT LAB and LENIA visuals no longer go blank and stay that way.
- AQUARIUM's tank is properly stocked.

### Fixed
- Text that was wrong, cut off, or never drawn, in many places.
- LITE OUT's bottom row was clipped.
- The level label in 3D MONSTR MAZE sat under the status line.
- A PAINT GIF fill could fill up the undo history.
- Painting names with accented letters showed as gaps.

## v1.0.1 — July 7, 2026

### Fixed
- Winning a game could freeze the cabinet.
- DEFENDR's smart bomb drained too fast. Also fixed: LITE OUT level
  progression, STACK streaks, and pawn promotion in CHESS.
- Nine Field Guide descriptions didn't match how the games actually play.
- The cabinet is now more resilient to power loss during an update, and to a
  bad release.

## v1.0 — June 8, 2026

First release for owners' cabinets.

- Updates install from the menu, and the menu shows faint amber lines when one
  is available. The INFO screen shows the installed version.
- **EARTH**: a rotating globe built from real map data.
- New scenes in TECTONIC and BRAIN, more shapes in CHLADNI, and more
  constellations, flags, weather, and DNA content.
- Hold a button to zoom in MOLECULES, PROTEINS, PEPTIDES, and LATTICES.
- TETROMINOS, ASTROIDS, and BREAK OUT are tuned to match the original arcade games.
- ROTARY: a two-lane roundabout. GREENWAVE cars now stop at red lights.
- Right-to-left scripts (Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Urdu, Pashto) display
  correctly in LANGUAGE.
